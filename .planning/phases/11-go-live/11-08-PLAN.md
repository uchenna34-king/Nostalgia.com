<objective>
Author `docs/PRE-LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md`: the single owner-facing runbook that enumerates every
placeholder and every deferred go-live action fenced out of Phase 11, so nothing that was
deliberately postponed is silently forgotten on launch day.

Phase 11 ends at *deployed and UAT-ready on Stripe TEST keys* (D-11), NOT at *taking real
money*. D-12 mandates that placeholder copy and deferred cutover steps are FLAGGED, not
written or performed, inside this phase. This plan is where that flag lives: a checklist the
owner works through on their explicit launch signal, each item saying exactly where the change
is made and what state it flips.

document converts every "not in this phase" item from the CONTEXT scope fence into an
actionable, located instruction, and records the D-06a email-delivery constraint as a stated
UAT limitation so testers hit it as documented behavior rather than a surprise bug.

Output:
- `docs/PRE-LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md` — a go-live actions section (one entry per deferred item, each
  with WHERE + WHAT) and a "Known UAT Limitation" section recording D-06a.

Scope fence: This plan writes a DOCUMENT only. It performs NONE of the actions it lists — it
does not swap keys, flip flags, publish the consent screen, buy a domain, or rewrite copy. It
adds no code and no runtime surface.
</objective>
