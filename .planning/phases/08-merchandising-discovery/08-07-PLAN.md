<objective>
Surface the wishlist across the storefront as a sibling of the cart (D-14, WISH-01):
a save toggle on every product card, a nav entry beside the cart with a count badge,
and a `/wishlist` page listing saved items. Because the wishlist stores a denormalized
item snapshot in context (08-04, mirroring the cart), the wishlist page renders
directly from `useWishlist()` with no extra server fetch — always available, no login,
persisted across sessions.

Purpose: Make the wishlist reachable and usable everywhere the customer browses.
Output: an updated `ProductCard`, an updated `Nav`, and a new `/wishlist` page.
</objective>
