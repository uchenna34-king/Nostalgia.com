<objective>
Make the storefront stock-aware (D-09). Two surfaces change: (1) the shared catalog `where`-builder in `lib/catalog.ts` hides any product whose sizes are ALL sold out, and (2) the PDP size selector disables sold-out sizes and blocks adding them to cart. The `?size=` filter is also flipped to stock-aware semantics, resolving the RESEARCH open question.

Purpose: D-09 requires that a fully sold-out product disappears from `/shop` and collection listings, and that a 0-stock size is disabled / marked "Sold out" on the PDP and cannot be added to cart. This is the cross-phase link that makes per-size inventory (built in 09-01) visible to customers. Because getCatalog is the single read path behind both `/shop` and every `/collections/[slug]` detail page, one added `AND` clause covers both — no new query path.

Scope boundary (D-10): stock is admin-managed only this phase — there is NO auto-decrement on checkout and NO server-side sold-out rejection at checkout. Blocking add-to-cart is a client concern here; server-side stock enforcement at checkout is deferred to Phase 11 (see threat model).

Output: a stock-aware `buildProductWhere`, a sold-out-aware `AddToCart`, and extended `tests/catalog.test.ts` coverage of the new clauses (closes the RESEARCH Wave 0 gap for ADMN-03).
</objective>
